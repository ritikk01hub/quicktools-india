Privacy Policy

Many QuickTools India tools perform calculations, text processing and image processing locally in your browser. Information entered into these tools is generally not sent to our server by the tool itself.

The QR Code Generator uses an external QR service and sends the text or URL you enter to that service to create the code. Do not enter passwords, private messages or other sensitive information into the QR tool.

GitHub Pages may process basic technical information as part of hosting the website. External services are governed by their own privacy policies. This page is general information and should be reviewed and updated to match the site's actual services before monetization.
